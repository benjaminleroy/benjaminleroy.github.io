Human Trafficking (Compressed)

Recruitment, transportation, transfer, harbouring of individuals by means of the threat or use of force or other forms of coercion for the purpose of exploitation (sexual exploitation, forced labour or services, slavery or practices similar to slavery or the removal of organs).

Annex II, Article 3 of the United Nations Convention Against Transnational Organized Crime and Protocols Thereto

Motivation

  • Human Interest
    • robs individuals of freedom and dignity
    • damages families
    • undermines rule of law
    • has been linked with other types of trans-national crime

Motivation and Background (Statistical Side)

  • Generally hard to get individual level data from around the globe on this subject
    • Difficulty recording individuals
    • Different countries = different definitions, gathering, etc
  • Hard to get good estimates on true size of problem
    • Media, NGOs, and Governments can sensationalize and misrepresent it
  • Hard to really understand risk factors

About

I am a second year PhD student in the Statistics Department at Carnegie Mellon University. I am currently working on batch corrections, imputation and cell proportion estimation for immunology data for my Advanced Data Analysis (ADA) project under Max G'Sell .

I was born and raised in Santa Rosa, California, one and a half hours drive north of San Francisco on the coast. I transferred from Santa Rosa Junior College (with AAs in Math and Economics) to University of Califoria, Berkeley where I obtained bachelor degrees in Statistics and Mathematics. During my undergraduate program I visited Carnegie Mellon to partipate in the Statistic Department's Summer Undergraduate Research Experience (SURE).

Review of DSI Fellowship 2018

2019/01/03

This last semester I had the privilege of serving as a Data Science Initiative (DSI) Fellow for the inaugural semester of the program in our Statistics and Data Science Department. Within this program groups of undergraduates work with outside companies on different questions of interests leveraging their course work to solve real world problems.

  • using github (especially through Rstudio)
  • communication tools
    • how to make good slide decks
    • consistent and readable graphics
    • clean posts
  • personal growth:
    • hard to be an advisor (when do you show, question, critique or stand back)
    • how much communication is needed?
    • with what frequency should meetings/ check-ins be at
    • how to be “one of many” advisors

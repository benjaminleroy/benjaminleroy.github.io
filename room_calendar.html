Private Meeting Rooms in FMS

Statistics and Data Science Department, Carnegie Mellon University

The following calendar and regulations related to the 2 private meeting rooms in FMS.

General Guidelines

  • Rooms are prioritized for interviews and conference calls
  • There are 2 rooms, so a maximum of 2 events can occur at once
  • Be respectful of other students, and also don't be too loud around the the rooms when you know they're in use

How to get access:

Contact your SAC rep and request that they add you to the calendar with event editing capacity. If you're a SAC rep/ new SAC rep you will get event editing and sharing capabilities. You will need to use your .andrew.cmu.edu account.

Future:

This style of calendar may be migrated to the new department website when that comes up.
